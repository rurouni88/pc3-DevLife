🏆 Win — Retire

              
Climb all four phases — Junior → Mid-Level → Senior → Staff/Principal — and defeat the final Staff/Principal boss to retire in style.

            

            

              
💀 Lose — Career Over

              
If any SPECIAL stat drops to rock bottom, your career is over:

              
    
                
  • Strength — Technical Collapse
    • 
                
  • Perception — Lost in the Stack
    • 
                
  • Endurance — Burnout
    • 
                
  • Charisma — Imposter Syndrome
    • 
                
  • Intelligence — Skill Obsolescence (after year one)
    • 
                
  • Agility — Velocity Zero
    • 
                
  • Luck — Bad Luck Runs Out
    • 
              

              
You can also be Made Redundant — low Charisma in a late career can get the axe to fall.

            

          

          

            
DevLife is a software-engineering roguelike. Build your SPECIAL stats, survive the daily grind, and climb the career ladder — or burn out trying.

            

            
Perks unlock when a base stat reaches 10. Max 1 equipment. Consumables are one-time use.
